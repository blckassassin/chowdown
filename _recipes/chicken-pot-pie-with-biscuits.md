ingredients:
- '### Chicken Pot Pie'
- 2 small boneless skinless chicken breasts
- Salt/pepper (to taste)
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 4 Tablespoons butter
- 1 cup onions (finely diced)
- 1 cup celery (finely diced)
- 1 cup carrots (finely diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- '½ teaspoon EACH: Onion Powder, Dry Thyme, Dry Rosemary'
- ¼ teaspoon ground sage
- 1/3 cup flour
- ½ cup half and half
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- 1 teaspoon low sodium soy sauce (can sub Worcestershire sauce)
- 1 cup frozen peas or 1 1/2 cups frozen green beans
directions:
- '### For the Biscuits'
- Preheat oven to 425 for homemade buttermilk biscuits or according to package for refrigerated biscuits.
- Prepare buttermilk biscuits but do not bake yet, store formed biscuits on a plate in the fridge while preparing the filling.
- Refrigerated biscuits may also be used, prepare them just before baking and refer to package instructions for time and temperature.
- '### For the Chicken Pot Pie'
- Season each side of the chicken with salt and pepper.
- Add chicken to a medium saucepan with chicken broth and bring to a very gentle bubble with the lid cracked for 15 minutes until cooked through.
- Do not boil rapidly or the chicken will be tough.
- Remove the chicken and shred using forks.
- Set aside the shredded chicken and reserve the broth.
- Melt butter in a wide (12-inch) pan over medium heat.
- Sauté onions, celery, and carrots for 5-6 minutes.
- Add garlic and seasonings, toss to coat, and cook for 1 more minute.
- Add flour and toss to coat, cooking for 2 minutes or until no raw flour smell remains.
- Add 2/3 of the chicken broth in small splashes, stirring to incorporate.
- Add the half and half in the same manner.
- Add chicken bouillon and soy sauce.
- Add the shredded chicken and stir to combine.
- If the gravy is not thick enough, bring to a gentle boil and reduce to a simmer.
- 'Optional: Add any of the remaining 1/3 chicken broth if desired.'
- Add the frozen peas or green beans and stir to combine and heat through.
- Remove from heat.
- Transfer filling to a lightly greased 8 x 8-inch baking dish if your skillet isn’t oven safe.
- Add the biscuits to the top and bake per biscuit recipe instructions until golden and set in the middle.
- 'Optional: Brush butter on top of the biscuits at the end and bake at 450° for 3-5 more minutes if additional browning is desired.'
