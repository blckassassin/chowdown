ingredients:
- 5 Tablespoons salted butter (plus additional for greasing pan)
- 2 cups milk chocolate chips
- ⅓ cup light corn syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 7 cups graham cracker cereal
- 15 oz mini marshmallows (This is 1 ½ 10 oz bags, you’ll need one full bag, then about 2 cups from the second bag)
directions:
- Lightly grease the sides and bottoms of a 9x13 baking dish with butter and set aside.
- In a large pot, combine butter, chocolate chips, and corn syrup over low heat.
- Stir frequently until the chocolate and butter are melted and the mixture is smooth.
- Add 10 oz mini marshmallows and vanilla extract, stirring frequently until the marshmallows are melted while keeping the heat low.
- Add graham cracker cereal and the remaining 5 oz mini marshmallows, stirring until completely coated with the chocolate mixture.
- Spread the mixture into the prepared baking dish and smooth the surface with a spatula.
- Sprinkle any reserved mini marshmallows over the surface of the cereal bars.
- Allow the bars to cool completely before slicing and serving.

These quick and easy S'mores Cereal Bars combine melted chocolate, butter, and marshmallows with graham cracker cereal for a fun summertime treat. They are simple to prepare and perfect for sharing.
