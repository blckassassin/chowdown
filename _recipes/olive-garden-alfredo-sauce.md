ingredients:
- 6 Tablespoons salted butter (high quality)
- 1 Tablespoon garlic (minced)
- 2 Tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 ½ cups heavy cream
- 1 ½ cups whole milk (1% or 2% can be used if needed)
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese (grated and at room temperature)
- ½ cup Romano cheese (grated and at room temperature)
- Salt and black pepper (to taste)
- 1 lb. Fettuccine
- Parsley (to garnish)
directions:
- Boil the fettuccine according to package instructions.
- Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat and add the garlic, cooking for one minute.
- Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring continuously.
- Add the heavy cream in splashes while stirring continuously, then add the milk in the same manner.
- Bring the mixture to a gentle bubble, then reduce heat to a simmer to allow it to thicken.
- Reduce heat to low and slowly stir in the cheese, then taste and season with salt and pepper if desired.
- Drain the pasta and combine it with the sauce until well incorporated, allowing the pasta to absorb the sauce.
- Garnish with parsley and serve.

This creamy homemade Alfredo sauce replicates the popular restaurant dish in just 20 minutes. It pairs perfectly with fettuccine for an easy and delicious family dinner.
