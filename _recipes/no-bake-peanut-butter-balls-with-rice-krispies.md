ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups no-stir creamy peanut butter (one 15 ounce jar)
- 3 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter (room temperature)
- 2 cups Rice Krispies
- 2 cups dark or semisweet chocolate chips (for dipping)
- 1/4 cup sprinkles (optional)
directions:
- In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, combine peanut butter and butter.
- Slowly add confectioner's sugar and then Rice Krispies, mixing until combined.
- Cover and chill the dough for 1 hour to firm it up and reduce stickiness.
- Scoop 1 tablespoon of dough and roll into 1-inch balls, placing them on a parchment or wax paper-lined baking sheet.
- Place the balls in the freezer for at least 15 minutes so they hold their shape during dipping.
- Place chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl and heat on 50% power, starting with 1 minute.
- Stir the chips and continue microwaving on 50% power, stirring every 15 seconds, until smooth.
- Dip the chilled balls in the melted chocolate and transfer them back to the lined baking sheet.
- Immediately add sprinkles if desired, then let the chocolate harden before serving or storing in an airtight container.

These easy no-bake treats combine creamy peanut butter, butter, sugar, and Rice Krispies for a salty-sweet snack. They are dipped in chocolate and optionally topped with sprinkles for a fun dessert.
