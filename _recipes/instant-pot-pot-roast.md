ingredients:
- 1 - 3 pounds (1176g) chuck roast or blade roast (, 2 inches thick)
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- 'Optional: 1 - 2 tablespoons (14g - 28g) unsalted butter or olive oil'
- 8 (160g) cremini mushrooms (, sliced)
- 2 (280g) small onions (, sliced)
- 6 (20g) garlic cloves (, minced)
- ¼ teaspoon (0.25g) dried rosemary
- ¼ teaspoon (0.35g) thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon) or balsamic vinegar
- 1 cup (250ml) unsalted chicken stock or high-quality unsalted beef stock
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) regular soy sauce
- 'Optional: 1 tablespoon (15ml) fish sauce (can substitute with regular soy sauce)'
- 2 - 4 (750g) potatoes (, quartered)
- 3 (280g) medium carrots (, cut into 1½ inch (4cm) chunks)
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper to taste
- '### Thickener'
- 2 tablespoons (18g) cornstarch mixed with 3 tablespoons (45ml) cold water
directions:
- Heat the Instant Pot to the Sauté More setting and wait until it indicates HOT.
- Pat the chuck roast dry and season one side generously with salt and pepper.
- Add olive oil to the pot and coat the bottom well.
- Place the roast in the pot seasoned side down and season the other side with salt and pepper.
- Brown the roast for 8 minutes per side, then remove and set aside.
- Add butter to the pot if using, then add the sliced mushrooms.
- Stir the mushrooms and cook until they release moisture and evaporate, then continue cooking until slightly crisped and browned.
- Add the sliced onions and sauté for 3 minutes until softened.
- Add the minced garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, and bay leaves, then sauté for another minute.
- Pour in the red wine or balsamic vinegar and deglaze the pot by scraping up the browned bits.
- Add the chicken stock, soy sauce, and fish sauce to the pot.
- Return the browned chuck roast to the pot, ensuring it is partially submerged in the liquid.
- Add the mushrooms to the pot if they were not cooked in it earlier.
- Close the lid, set the venting knob to Sealing, and pressure cook on High for 45 minutes.
- Allow a Full Natural Release for 25 minutes, then open the lid carefully.
- Remove the roast and cover it with aluminum foil to rest.
- Submerge the quartered potatoes in the sauce and stack the carrot chunks on top.
- Close the lid again, set the venting knob to Sealing, and pressure cook on High for 4 minutes.
- Perform a Quick Release, then open the lid carefully.
- Taste the sauce and adjust seasoning if necessary.
- Mix the cornstarch and cold water, then stir it into the sauce one third at a time until the desired thickness is reached.
- Slice the pot roast against the grain and serve with the gravy and vegetables.
