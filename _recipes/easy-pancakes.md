ingredients:
- 100g plain flour
- 2 large eggs
- 300ml milk
- 1 tbsp sunflower or vegetable oil plus a little extra for frying
- lemon wedges to serve (optional)
- caster sugar to serve (optional)
directions:
- Whisk 100g plain flour, 2 large eggs, 300ml milk, 1 tbsp sunflower or vegetable oil, and a pinch of salt in a bowl or jug until smooth, resembling single cream.
- Let the batter rest for 30 minutes if time permits, or proceed immediately.
- Heat a medium frying pan or crêpe pan over medium heat and wipe with oiled kitchen paper.
- Cook each pancake for 1 minute on each side using about half a ladleful of batter until golden.
- Keep cooked pancakes warm in a low oven while finishing the batch.
- Serve with lemon wedges and caster sugar or your preferred filling.
- To freeze, layer cooled pancakes between baking parchment, wrap in cling film, and store for up to two months.

These foolproof crêpes are simple to make and result in light, golden pancakes perfect for any meal. They can be served immediately with lemon and sugar or frozen for later.
