ingredients:
- '### Crumb Topping'
- 1/2 cup (63g) all-purpose flour (spooned & leveled)
- 1/2 cup (100g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1 and 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/4 cup (4 Tablespoons; 56g) unsalted butter, cold
- '### Pumpkin Coffee Cake'
- 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour (spooned & leveled)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 and 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg*
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves*
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger*
- 1 cup (226g) pumpkin puree
- 1/2 cup (100g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (120ml) canola or vegetable oil (or melted coconut oil if you prefer)
- 1/4 cup (80g/60ml) pure maple syrup
- 1/4 cup (60ml) milk*
- '### Vanilla Icing'
- 1 cup (120g) confectioners’ sugar
- 2 Tablespoons pumpkin spice coffee creamer, half-and-half, or milk
- 1/4 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
directions:
- Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and grease a 9-inch square or springform pan with nonstick spray.
- Whisk the flour, brown sugar, and cinnamon together in a medium bowl for the topping.
- Cut in the cold butter using a pastry blender or fork until the mixture forms clumps and crumbs, then set aside.
- Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and ginger together in a large bowl.
- Whisk the pumpkin, brown sugar, oil, maple syrup, and milk together in a separate medium bowl.
- Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined, being careful not to over-mix.
- Spoon the thick batter into the prepared pan and spread it into an even surface.
- Pour the crumb topping evenly over the batter and gently press it down.
- Bake for 30-35 minutes, checking doneness at 30 minutes with a toothpick.
- Whisk the icing ingredients together until smooth, adding extra liquid if needed to thin.
- Drizzle the icing over the warm or cooled cake before serving.
